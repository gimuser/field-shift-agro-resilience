# NASA Datasets

## Candidate sources to investigate
- NASA POWER
- SMAP soil moisture
- GPM IMERG precipitation
- MODIS vegetation observations
- ECOSTRESS evapotranspiration

## Required investigation for each dataset
- Official documentation and access endpoint
- Variables and units
- Spatial and temporal resolution
- Historical coverage
- Update frequency
- Geographic coverage
- Licensing and attribution
- Known limitations and uncertainty
- Suitability for the proposed agricultural use

## Important
Candidate datasets are not yet approved for the application.
Do not treat a proxy or modeled estimate as a direct measurement.
