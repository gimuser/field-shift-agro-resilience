# Agronomic Methodology

## Research questions
- Which soil-health indicators can be assessed?
- Which crop characteristics are relevant?
- How do crop rotations affect soil nutrients and water demand?
- How should climate risks influence crop selection?
- Which recommendations require local expert validation?

## Methodology requirements
- Document every variable and its source.
- Distinguish observations, estimates, and assumptions.
- Explain scoring and weighting.
- Represent uncertainty.
- Avoid unsupported yield or soil-health predictions.
